Understanding the Mission

What Is GetLoud? The Story & Purpose

GetLoud is a grassroots endurance and creative movement bridging Perth, Western Australia, with mental health initiatives in Nepal.

5km Daily Endurance

Karsang Ghising will run 5km every single day for 100 consecutive days around Perth WA (500km total) without taking a single day off, symbolizing the constant persistence needed in mental health recovery.

14 Weekly Collaborations

Every week of the 14-week campaign is paired with a guest collaborator—artists, musicians, dancers, and runners—who donate $20, run 5km in Perth with Karsang, and co-host a mental health podcast.

Music & Expression

As frontman of Perth band Snowcoal and founder of Krash Productions, Karsang uses original songs and media production to turn unexpressed pain into powerful artistic expression.

Direct Crisis Funding

100% of public donations and collaborator contributions go directly to verified non-profit mental health partners funding 24/7 suicide crisis helplines and youth tele-counseling.

The Event Mechanics

How The 100-Day Run Operates

From Perth running tracks to global Strava participation—here is how the campaign is structured.

01

Daily 5km Strava Logging in Perth

Every single day, Karsang completes a 5km run across Perth routes (Kings Park, Swan River, Matagarup Bridge, Cottesloe Beach) and logs it live on Strava for 100% verification.

02

Weekly Collaborator Registration ($20)

Supporters and creatives select one of the 14 open weekly slots, contribute a $20 donation, and schedule their 5km run + podcast recording session with Karsang in Perth.

03

Unscripted Mental Health Podcast

After the 5km run, Karsang and the guest record an episode discussing emotional resilience, creativity, coping mechanisms, and breaking stigma.

04

Direct Disbursement & Impact

All funds collected are transferred directly to verified non-profit mental health partners providing free tele-counseling hours and emergency helpline support.
